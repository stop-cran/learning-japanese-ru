The revised 太 title is `толстый в обхвате; полный; великий (например, в слове
со значением «солнце»)`; it is nonexclusive and does not print 太 or 太陽.
Kanji Pedia sense ① says `おおきい` and lists both 太洋 and 太陽
(https://www.kanjipedia.jp/kanji/0004424200). This supports an example of a
word meaning “sun” for the large/great sense; it does not mean that 太 by
itself means “sun”. The lesson also cautions against reading 太陽 as “thick
sun”.
