Findings:
1. Moderate; comprehensibility; words/とにかく.md:19, contradicted by the note at :21; «Не говоря уже о цене, качество хорошее» teaches “not to mention / let alone”, so the price is added as a further positive. The card’s own note says Nはとにかく names what is set aside, and the English is “Leaving the price aside, the quality is good.” A learner who copies the Russian example uses the construction for the opposite discourse move. Remedy: «Цена — вопрос отдельный, а качество хорошее.» or «О цене умолчим: качество хорошее.» Evidence: JMdict 1443990 sense 2, “setting aside …; regardless of …”, as `...は〜`; the Russian idiom does not mean that. Confidence: high.
2. Moderate; comprehensibility; words/たっぷり.md:18, with a degree overstatement at :19; «побольше воды» is “a little more water”, not the card’s “вдоволь; обильно”, the English “plenty of water”, or JMdict 1007240 sense 1 “plentifully; amply; abundantly; generously”. A learner will under-dose たっぷり. «уйма времени» is colloquial “a huge mass”, stronger than “plenty”, same direction but overstated. «вволю отдохнуть» at :25 matches вдоволь and is not part of the defect. Remedy: «Налейте, пожалуйста, в кастрюлю воды вдоволь.» and «До отправления времени у нас вдоволь.» Evidence: Kuznetsov (Грамота), побольше = «несколько, немного больше»; Kuznetsov/Ozhegov, уйма = разг. большое количество, множество. Confidence: high on :18; medium-high that :19 is an overstatement rather than a wrong direction.
3. Moderate; comprehensibility; words/それぞれ.md:4, :12, :16 and :19; «соответственно» means likewise, as required, or according to, not “respectively”. «живут соответственно в Токио и Осаке» reads as “they live accordingly in Tokyo and Osaka”, so the pairing of brother/Tokyo and sister/Osaka disappears. Line 16 states the pairing, but the gloss a learner will memorize does not. Remedy: title «каждый (из нескольких); каждому своё»; example «Старший брат живёт в Токио, а младшая сестра — в Осаке.» Evidence: Kuznetsov (Грамота): равным образом; надлежащим образом; предлог «согласно»; particle «да». No respectively sense. Confidence: high.
4. Minor; comprehensibility; words/たまらない.md:4 and :11, against the card’s own gloss at :32; «неотразимый» is Kuznetsov sense 1, an attack or argument that cannot be repelled, and sense 2, something captivating (impression, charm, beauty). It does not cover «невозможно сдержать / невозможно оторваться», nor unbearable heat. A learner can store たまらない as “charming”. «невыносимый» is fine. Remedy: title «невыносимый; невозможно сдержать». If a charm gloss stays, qualify it against line 32. Evidence: Kuznetsov (Грамота), неотразимый; the card’s positive example does not use the title word. Confidence: high that the gloss is narrower; medium that a reader of the body will still mislearn it.
5. Minor; comprehensibility; words/せい.md:27; «Категория канго следует этому разбору, не считая предложенное происхождение несомненным» is an author-facing classification note. «канго» and the reading しょい are unexplained, so a learner may think しょい must be learned in order to use せい. The attachment chart and the おかげ/原因 contrast are intact; this is not a wrong usage rule. Remedy: delete that sentence. If the hedged etymology stays, say in learner terms that the dictionary’s proposed link is uncertain and not needed for use. Evidence: the sentence discusses the card’s type category, not how to use the word. Confidence: high that it is author-facing; medium that it harms learning rather than merely distracting.
