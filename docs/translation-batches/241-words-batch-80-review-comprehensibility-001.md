Findings:
C1; medium; comprehensibility; words/一段.md:4 and :19; «ещё заметнее» teaches “more noticeably”, not 一段と “even more / to a further degree”. The example 一段と寒くなりました becomes “похолодало ещё заметнее” (the cold grew more perceptible) instead of “became even colder”. A learner will treat the adverb as about noticeability. The same line already has the broader gloss «в ещё большей степени», so the title and example should lead with that. Remedy: title and H1 `ещё больше; одна ступень`; example `После дождя стало ещё холоднее.` Exclusion with 一層/更に already exists. Evidence: Japanese 寒くなりました is “became cold”; EN “it became even colder.” Confidence: high.
C2; medium; comprehensibility; words/ばからしい.md:4 and :20; Title `нелепый; смехотворный` drops the sense the opening teaches («глупое… или не стоящее серьёзного отношения»). «смехотворный» is bookish and pulls the quiz answer toward 傑作’s humorous absurdity. The rumor example renders ばからしい as «нелепо», so the foolish/not-worth-it sense never appears in the answer or the examples. Remedy: title and H1 `глупый; нелепый`; example `Верить таким слухам глупо.` Exclusion with 馬鹿 already exists. Evidence: body line 16; EN title “absurd; foolish; ridiculous” and “It is foolish to believe…”. Confidence: high.
C3; medium; comprehensibility; words/余計.md:4 and :21; Third title segment «сильнее прежнего» narrows “all the more” to “stronger than before”. The next example is “ещё труднее” (harder, no “than before”), so the quiz label does not match the taught adverb. The first example drops てしまう and adds «совсем»: 言ってしまいました is “ended up saying”, not “said something that was completely unnecessary”. Remedy: title and H1 `сверх необходимого; ненужный; тем более`; example `Я в итоге сказал лишнее.` Evidence: Japanese on line 21; EN “I ended up saying something unnecessary” and title “all the more”. Confidence: high.
C4; medium; comprehensibility; words/広める.md:4; «расширять (знания)» makes the second sense knowledge-only. Line 17 teaches a general “расширять, увеличивать охват”; knowledge is only the second example. Remedy: title and H1 `распространять; расширять`. Evidence: EN title “disseminate; broaden”; body “to broaden or extend”. Confidence: high.
C5; medium; comprehensibility; words/大層.md:4 and :21; «изрядно» is colloquial (“quite a bit / thoroughly”), but line 45 says the word can sound more formal or old-fashioned than とても. «раздутый (о словах)» is not a natural gloss for exaggerated speech (ordinary sense: inflated, swollen) and is not the word the body uses («преувеличенном», «напыщенная»). いただきました is humble receipt; «Мне преподнесли» makes unnamed givers the agents and adds a ceremonial presentation. Confidence: high.
C6; medium; comprehensibility; words/うんと.md:4; Opening line 16 separates “большое количество или высокую степень” from the comparison use in the next sentence. Exclusions with とても and 非常に already exist. Evidence: card line 16 versus line 17; EN title “a lot; very much”. Confidence: high.

Remedy: `Я об этом вовсе так не думаю.` Evidence: Japanese なんとも思いません; EN “I do not care about that at all” still does not license “волновать”. Confidence: high.
