EN title is `high-rise; upper atmosphere`, and the EN example is `a high-rise building`. Confidence: high.
- C2; Moderate; comprehensibility; `words/飾り.md:4` (also 12, 16); «орнамент» is a calque of English `ornament`. In Russian it is a repeating decorative pattern, not a Christmas decoration or a small charm on a hat. The meaning sentence defines орнамент as «то, что украшает», which the examples then translate as «украшения» / «украшение», not as an орнамент. Impact: the quiz gloss teaches a different object from the examples. Remedy: title and H1 `украшение`; meaning sentence `飾り — украшение: то, что украшает предмет, человека или место.` Do not add a note that merely rejects «орнамент» after removing it. Evidence: Gramota, Большой толковый словарь Кузнецова and Бабенко: ритмически чередующийся узор, not a hung decoration (https://gramota.ru/meta/ornament). EN `ornament` is the source of the calque; the hat example there is `a small ornament`, which RU already renders as «маленькое украшение». Confidence: high.
- C3; Moderate; comprehensibility; `words/騒々しい.md:4` (also 12); «галдящий» is an unqualified lead gloss. The article never uses it. Its own definition is «шумную, крикливую, полную гама», the voice gloss is «громкий, крикливый голос», and both examples are translated «так шумно» / «шумно и суетно». Impact: the quiz answer is a colloquial word for people talking loudly all at once, and it does not cover a loud voice, a noisy room, or the unsettled-atmosphere sense already glossed separately. Remedy: title and H1 `крикливый; беспокойный (об обстановке)`. Avoid leading with «шумный»: that is the lead quiz gloss of `words/うるさい.md`. Evidence: Ozhegov, галдеть: «Громко говорить, орать (преимущ. о многих)», simple/disapproving (https://gufo.me/dict/ozhegov/%D0%B3%D0%B0%D0%BB%D0%B4%D0%B5%D1%82%D1%8C, definition as returned in search).
