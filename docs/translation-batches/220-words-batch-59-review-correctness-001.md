Findings:

C1; Minor; correctness; words/一人一人.md:30.
Claim and impact: The added comparison with 個人 says 一人一人 "перебирает всех членов группы по очереди". The unqualified "по очереди" makes sequential order part of the word's meaning. That contradicts the retained explanation at line 34: 一人一人の意見 does not specify speaking order. A learner can incorrectly infer that this phrase excludes simultaneous or unordered individual contributions.
Remedy: Replace the final clause with "一人一人 же выделяет каждого члена группы в отдельности, не обязательно задавая порядок".
Evidence: Digital Daijisen, 一人一人, sense 1: 多くの中のそれぞれの人。めいめい。各人; its example 一人一人の自覚が大切だ concerns each person's awareness, not a sequence (https://kotobank.jp/word/一人一人-611810). JMdict/Jisho sense 1 gives "each (person); individually" alongside contextual "one by one; in turn" (https://jisho.org/api/v1/search/words?keyword=一人一人). The complete EN original, line 32, explicitly states that a particular speaking order is not required.
Confidence: high. Status: unresolved at the reviewed candidate.
