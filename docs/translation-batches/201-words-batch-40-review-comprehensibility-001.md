Findings:
F1; minor; comprehensibility; words/燃える.md:16; «глагол группы ичидан» is not the term used everywhere else, so a learner who has met «итидан» can treat this as a different class. The forms 燃えます / 燃えて / 燃えない are given later and are not themselves wrong. Remedy: replace the clause with «непереходный глагол итидан» (same pattern as words/焼ける.md:16 and words/あふれる.md:16). Evidence: RU glossary, docs/translation-methodology.md, row 一段動詞 = «глагол итидан»; corpus uses итидан, not ичидан. This is also a Cyrillic rendering of いち via English ichi, not the prescribed term. Confidence: high.
F2; minor; comprehensibility; words/犯罪.md:25; «действенный» состав does not mean «action-like». In ordinary Russian действенный means effective / producing an effect, so the sentence fails to say that the kanji suggest an action while the word taught here is a noun. The noun label itself is still explicit, and the examples do not treat 犯罪 as a verb. Remedy: «Несмотря на то, что состав напоминает о действии, изучаемое здесь слово — существительное.» Evidence: EN words/犯罪.md, «Even with this action-like composition, the word taught here is a noun.» No Japanese sense is in dispute; JMdict 1481590 sense 1 remains the noun «crime». Confidence: high.
