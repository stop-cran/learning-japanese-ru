## Semantic findings and qualified associations

* **州 / 川:** The captions “штат; провинция” and “река” are distinct. 州's candidate lesson explicitly separates the administrative sense from the native noun す “sandbar” and warns against transferring it to every place name (`candidate-root\州.md` L18–20, L31–45). The published 川 lesson explains 州 as land/sandbars or islands in a river (`comparison-root\川.md` L18–22). This is a real geographic/polysemy association, present whenever 州 and 川 co-occur (nine of the 54 contexts, including 州/川, 州/形, and 州/卵 contexts in each applicable profile), but the shown captions still distinguish province/state from river. No competing displayed Russian answer was found. Visual similarity and learner confusion are not assessed.
* **帯 / 帽:** “пояс; зона” versus “шапка; головной убор” are semantically distinct, though both concern clothing/headwear and share 巾. They co-occur in current-starter 帯/帽, all three current-inclusive-n2 帯 sets, and legacy 帯/帽 (`candidate-root\帯.md` L18–20; `candidate-root\帽.md` L18–20). The domain/shape association is not a caption ambiguity. The corresponding drawings are not reviewed.
* **帽 / 幅; 幅 / 福 / 富:** The meanings “hat,” “width/breadth,” “good fortune,” and “wealth/abundance” stay distinct in all sets. The lessons themselves caution that the similar right-side shapes do not imply shared meaning (`candidate-root\帽.md` L40–42; `candidate-root\幅.md` L18–20, L37–39; `comparison-root\福.md` L20 and `富.md` L20). Shape association is visual, not evidence of semantic ambiguity. All are actual co-displays in inclusive sets; the repeated sets were assessed as full contexts, not as isolated pairs.
* **巻 / 券:** “рулон; том книги” and “билет; купон” do not overlap semantically in these options. There is a genuine Japanese reading association: 巻 includes ケン in 席巻, while 券 is read ケン (`candidate-root\巻.md` L9, L27–30, L45–46; `comparison-root\券.md` L9, L28–34). The Russian-caption observations do not test a spoken ケン prompt or voice recognition.
* **幅 / 福:** Both have an on-reading フク in their lessons, and they co-occur in the starter pair plus the repeated inclusive contexts. For 幅 the candidate teaches はば and 増幅 ぞうふく; it does not teach a spoken standalone 幅 ふく scroll noun/counter (`candidate-root\幅.md` L18–20, L26–38; `comparison-root\福.md` L9, L28–29). ## 幅 metadata-to-lesson scroll-sense coverage

The Russian candidate teaches 幅 はば as width/breadth and its range/difference extension, plus 増幅 ぞうふく; the pinned English lesson teaches the same scope and distinguishes 幅 from 広い. Neither lesson explicitly teaches the reported scroll noun/counter (candidate `candidate-root\幅.md` L18–20, L26–39; English `english-root\幅.md` L18–20, L26–38). The retained evidence note reports, but did not independently refetch, 2026-11-13 Jiten observations: 幅 はば rank #5923 / 4355 media, 幅 ふく (scroll noun/counter) #183956 / 15, and 一幅 いっぷく #72466 / 166 (`haba-evidence` L27–48). It also reports a separate dictionary attestation of width, scroll noun, and counter, not independently checked in this review (`haba-evidence` L52–58).

This evidence is insufficient to establish a population frequency, sense-specific prevalence, or a curriculum requirement to include or omit the scroll sense. The lower reported media rank is not a coverage threshold, the values are time-bound and not refetched, and the counts cannot be combined into a sense denominator. The author's recommendation to retain the current candidate is not my verdict and does not settle the question (`haba-recommendation` L1–8, L37–53, L63–68). **Finding:** the omission is a real metadata-to-lesson scope question; whether the secondary sense belongs in this lesson remains **UNVERIFIED**, not a demonstrated semantic defect.
