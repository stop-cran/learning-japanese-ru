2. Minor; comprehensibility and scoped coverage; words/みじめ.md:4,12,19. The title and first translation require humiliation («жалкий и униженный»). Daijisen is かわいそうで見るにしのびないさま / いたいたしいさま; JMdict 1303280 is miserable/wretched/unhappy/sad/pitiable. EN title is miserable/pitiable/wretched, and its example is only «I felt miserable».
