Findings:
- C236-01; medium; correctness; words/誓う.md:41–42. «Русское “клясться” в разговорной речи может означать и “браниться, проклинать”» transfers English “swear” polysemy to a different Russian lemma. Kuznetsov defines клясться as giving an oath/emphatically promising; the curse/reproach meanings belong to клясть. This teaches an erroneous Russian/Japanese distinction. Remedy: remove this English-only contrast, or replace both sentences with «誓う означает давать клятву или обет, а не браниться». Evidence: https://gramota.ru/poisk?query=клясться&mode=slovari (Kuznetsov, actual entry); https://gufo.me/dict/ozhegov/клясть (displayed Kuznetsov meanings 1–2 and academic-dictionary meanings distinguish the nonreflexive lemma); JMdict 1381210, sense 1, https://www.edrdg.org/pub/Nihongo/JMdict_e.gz. Confidence high; unresolved.
- C236-02; medium; scoped coverage; words/請う.md:4,12. Title «просить, испрашивать (наставления, прощения)» drops the EN title's primary “invite” sense, still explicitly taught at RU lines 16,29,37,42. The qualifier also confines the title to two request objects while EN covers requests generally. Remedy: «просить, умолять; приглашать» in front matter and heading (retain formal construction explanation in the body). Evidence: frozen EN words/請う.md:4,16,38–48; actual JMdict-backed entry https://jisho.org/api/v1/search/words?keyword=%E8%AB%8B%E3%81%86, sense 1 (“to beg; to ask; to request; to invite”), confirmed in JMdict XML 1592920. Confidence high; unresolved.
- C236-03; low; correctness; words/話し合い.md:19. «Завтра мы всей семьёй всё обсудим» adds an all-inclusive object (“everything”) absent from 明日、家族で話し合いをします and EN “We will have a family discussion tomorrow.” 話し合い expresses discussion, not its exhaustive scope. This is a translation-content addition, not an objection to rendering a Japanese noun with a Russian verb. Remedy: «Завтра мы обсудим это всей семьёй». Evidence: Japanese/EN example, frozen EN words/話し合い.md:19; JMdict 1600910, sense 1 (“discussion; talk; tête-à-tête; conference”), https://www.edrdg.org/pub/Nihongo/JMdict_e.gz. Confidence high; unresolved.
