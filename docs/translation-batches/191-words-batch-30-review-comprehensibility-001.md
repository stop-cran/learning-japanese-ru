C2; Minor; comprehensibility; words/販売.md:40-41; Learner text opens with "значения английского sale". The useful Russian point is already in the same sentence. Impact: the first clause is not followable without English. Remedy: replace lines 40-43 with: "Не принимайте 販売 за «распродажу»: в магазине оно может означать продажу по обычной цене. Evidence: JMdict 1481800 sense 1 is sale/sales/selling/marketing, not a discount event; the Russian «распродажа» warning is the adaptation that should remain. C3; Minor; comprehensibility; words/貢献.md:43-44; "Если в английском толковании встретится services..." plus "речь о заслугах" depends on an English gloss the learner never sees, and "заслуги" is not the card's "вклад". Evidence: JMdict 1282410 sense 1 is "contribution (furthering a goal or cause); services (to a cause)". Russian «вклад» does not create a customer-service reading. Confidence: high.
C4; Minor; comprehensibility; words/負け.md:20,23; 今回は私の負けです is translated "На этот раз я проиграл", then line 23 says the example does not use a past-tense verb. Impact: the noun-vs-verb contrast with 負ける is contradicted at the first example. Line 41 later gives the better gloss «это мой проигрыш». Remedy: line 20 translation «На этот раз это мой проигрыш.» Evidence: the Japanese is noun + non-past です; "проиграл" is past. Confidence: high.

Confidence: high that the contrast is absent; medium that learners are misled once the exclusion is in place.
