F1 – RU-introduced quiz_exclusions (7 additions on 6 cards), each declared on one side only. Dictionary basis, and whether necessity is established:
- 活躍 → 役 «роль; обязанность» and 活躍 → 役割 «роль; круг обязанностей»: **required (given the current RU title).** The RU title «заметная роль» puts the word «роль» into 活躍, which EN's title ("energetic activity; active contribution") does not have, and the card's own example and the sentence «Xとして活躍する указывает роль» teach the role frame. Basis: the Russian title segment «роль» is identical to the correct answer of 役 and nearly identical to 役割 (JMdict 役 "role; duty", 役割 "role; part"). A learner who sees 役 with 活躍's title as an option can choose on the shared word. Keep both. (See P1 for the title itself.)
- 能率 → 効果 «эффект; действенность»: **required.** 能率 «эффективность (работы)» against 効果 «действенность» (JMdict 効果 "effect; effectiveness"; 能率 "efficiency"). «Эффективность» and «действенность» are near-synonyms in Russian. EN already links 効果 from 能率 and the body contrasts them, so the pair is defensible both ways. Keep.
- 育児 → 育てる «растить; воспитывать»: **conservative (real basis, necessity unestablished).** JMdict 育てる sense 1 "to raise (children)" overlaps with 育児 «воспитание детей». The parts of speech differ (noun vs verb), so a quiz is unlikely to put them side by side; EN has no list on 育児. Keep: harmless and cheap.
- 肯定 → 主張 «утверждение; настаивание на своём»: **conservative.** Shared stem «утвер» (утвердительное суждение / утверждение). JMdict 肯定 "affirmation; positive" against 主張 "claim; assertion; insistence". The meanings are distinct (agreeing vs asserting), but the Russian words are near each other, so the guard has a real lexical basis. Necessity is not established. Keep.
- 職人 → 名人 «мастер своего дела»: **conservative.** JMdict 名人 "master; expert; virtuoso" against 職人 "craftsman; artisan". The shared word «мастер» appears in both titles («мастер ручного труда» / «мастер своего дела»). The senses differ (trade vs. exceptional skill), but a learner could defend the overlap. Keep.
- 習字 → 清書 «чистовик; переписывание начисто»: **conservative, leaning unjustified.** The basis is only the stem «чист-» (чистописание / чистовик, переписывание начисто) and the school-writing topic. 清書 is a "fair copy" and 習字 is practice in forming characters, so no defensible-answer overlap is established, and the titles already separate the cards. Harmless over-guard; may stay or go (nonblocking).
None of the seven guards the antonym or contrast that the article itself teaches, and none is unjustified in the sense of mere topical relation, except the weak 習字→清書 above. Over-guarding is limited to that one entry.
