Findings:

C1. Medium. Comprehensibility. `words/生ずる.md:4` (repeated at line 12; body line 16). The quiz gloss «вызывать» is the same word as `words/呼ぶ.md` title «звать; вызывать», and neither card excludes the other. On 呼ぶ the body uses it for summoning a person («окликать, звать к себе или вызывать кого-либо»); on 生ずる it means to cause. A learner cannot tell the answers apart. JMdict 生ずる (jisho slug 生ずる) is "to produce, to yield, to cause" / "to result from, to arise, to be generated"; «порождать» already covers the transitive sense, and the second example already shows it without this word. Remedy: title and H1 `возникать; порождать`. Line 16: `生ずる означает порождать что-либо либо возникать, зарождаться.` Do not keep «вызывать» and add a quiz exclusion: the exclusion would hide the pair, not tell the senses apart. Confidence: high.

C2. Medium. Comprehensibility. `words/瓶詰.md:4` (H1 line 12; meaning line 16; example line 20; synonym line 38). «Розлив по бутылкам и банкам; в бутылках или банках» is too long for one phone quiz answer, and «розлив» is industrial pouring of liquid. Digital Daijisen (Weblio, びん‐づめ) is 瓶につめること。また、瓶につめたもの。 JMdict 1491130 is "bottling" (noun, suru) / "bottled" (の). EN's own example is "I put the jam into jars," not a pour. «Я разлил джем по банкам» follows the calque and is natural only for hot, pourable jam. «банка» beside 缶's «металлическая банка; жестянка» is already clear in the synonym section and does not need to be solved by «розлив». Do not use «укупорка»: the card correctly says sealing is not guaranteed. Remedy: title and H1 `фасовка в бутылки и банки; в бутылке или банке`. Line 16: `瓶詰 означает фасовку в бутылки или банки, а 瓶詰する — помещать что-либо`. Line 20: `Я разложил джем по банкам.` Line 38: `Фасовка может быть частью`. No existing title uses «фасовка». Confidence: high.

C3. Medium. Comprehensibility. `words/独り言.md:4` (H1 line 12; line 17 contradicts line 41). Unqualified «монолог» in ordinary Russian is a speech by one person, often to listeners. Line 41 says a speech to an audience is not automatically 独り言. JMdict 1455670 sense 1 is "soliloquy, monologue, speaking to oneself"; the English dictionary word does not license the unqualified Russian one. The first gloss and both examples are already the right sense. Remedy: title and H1 `разговор с самим собой; монолог (к себе)`. Line 17: `Так называют и монолог, если это речь к самому себе, а не к слушателям.` Confidence: high.

C4. Medium. Comprehensibility. `words/特定.md:4` (H1 line 12; meaning line 17; example line 20; nuance line 44). EN title is "specific; identifying; specifying". JMdict 特定 sense 2 is "specifying, designating, identifying, pinpointing". The Russian meaning line teaches all three verbs («указать, выявить или точно установить»), and line 44 contrasts identifying a cause with deliberately designating a product. The title keeps only the adjective and narrows the verb to «выявление (причины, лица)»: the first example is products, and no example is a person. «конкретный» does not collide with 具体's «конкретность; воплощение (идеи)» (already excluded). Do not reuse 指定's «указание; установление» or 決める's «определять»; «определение» also stems with 者's «с определением». The specifying sense is still in the body, so this is not a coverage loss. Remedy: title and H1 `конкретный; выявление; обозначение`. Confidence: high.
