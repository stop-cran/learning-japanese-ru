Findings:
1. Moderate; comprehensibility and coverage; words/障子.md:19; «Я заменил бумагу на сёдзи» is заменить A на B, so the learner is told the paper was replaced by a shoji. That reverses 障子の紙を張り替えました and fights the next sentence, which says the covering is changed, not the frame. Remedy: «Я переклеил бумагу на сёдзи.» Evidence: JMdict 張り替える/貼り替える (jisho) sense 1 «to re-cover; to repaper»; EN 6af98cb «I replaced the paper on the shoji». Confidence: high.
2. Moderate; comprehensibility and coverage; words/開通.md:19-20, against :16 and :32; 開通する is taught as intransitive (subject, not を), but «Телефонную линию подключили» and «Дорогу … снова открыли» are agentive. The third example is worse than EN, which has intransitive «reopened». A learner can take the forbidden 道路を開通する pattern from the Russian. Remedy: «Телефонная линия заработала.» and «Дорога, которая была перекрыта, снова открылась для движения.» Evidence: JMdict 開通 (jisho) senses 1-2, both Noun/する, intransitive; EN third gloss «The road that had been closed to traffic reopened». Confidence: high.
3. Moderate; comprehensibility and coverage; words/締め切る.md:19, against :16; the card says this is a transitive godan verb, but «Приём заявок на участие закончится завтра» is intransitive and has no closer. EN keeps the agent: «We will close applications». Remedy: «Завтра мы закроем приём заявок на участие.» Evidence: JMdict 締め切る (jisho) senses 1-2, godan, transitive; card's own 申し込みを締め切った note. Confidence: high.
4. Moderate; comprehensibility and coverage; words/防犯.md:18; «Чтобы защититься от преступников» turns crime prevention into personal self-defense against criminals. The title and the 防犯のために gloss in the same section still say prevention of crimes. Remedy: «Чтобы предотвратить преступления, я на ночь всегда запираю дверь.» Evidence: JMdict 防犯 (jisho) sense 1 «prevention of crime»; EN «To prevent crime, I always lock up at night». Confidence: high.
5. Moderate; comprehensibility; words/期限.md:4 and :12; lead quiz gloss «срок действия» is the ordinary validity period of a document, contract, or medicine. The examples are an application deadline and a return deadline, and the body says a set term or the moment it ends. EN leads with deadline/term/time limit. Remedy: title and H1 «установленный срок; крайний срок». Evidence: JMdict 期限 (jisho) sense 1 «term; period; time frame», sense 2 «time limit; deadline; cutoff (date)»; neither sense is led by a validity-period label. Confidence: high.

Minor; comprehensibility and coverage; words/関わる.md:18, against :29; «Я участвую в новом проекте» uses the verb the same card assigns to 参加する and hides the contrast it has just drawn (причастность is not necessarily участие). 参加's title is «участие». Remedy: «Я вовлечён в новый проект.» Evidence: card line 29; EN «I am involved in the new project». JMdict 関わる sense 1 also lists «to take part», so this is a failed contrast, not an impossible sense. Confidence: high that the example fails the card's own contrast; medium that a dictionary would forbid the verb.
7. Minor; comprehensibility; words/隔てる.md:4 and :12; title «стоять между» adds standing and sounds intransitive. The body (:16) says «находиться или иметь что-то между», and the examples use «разделяет» and «сели … через стол», not standing. Remedy: title and H1 «отгораживать; иметь между собой». Evidence: card cites JMdict 1206360 senses 1-3; EN title «separate; have between». Confidence: high.
8. Minor; comprehensibility; words/締め切り.md:16; «написание из исходного списка» is an author note. A beginner has no source list. Remedy: «**締切** и **締切り** передают то же слово с тем же чтением.» Evidence: the phrase names an editorial list, not a spelling fact; EN has the same note, which does not make it learner Russian. Confidence: high.
