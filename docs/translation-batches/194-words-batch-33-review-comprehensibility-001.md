- C1; Minor; comprehensibility; words/被害.md:16; The lead definition says the harm was «увиденные со стороны того, кто их понёс». «Увиденные» is the past participle of «увидеть» (completed visual perception), so a beginner can read 被害 as damage that was seen or witnessed, not as damage suffered. The examples («пострадали», «ущерба не было») recover the intended sense, so the card is not unusable, but the opening sentence is the definition. Remedy: replace with «被害 — ущерб, повреждение или вред со стороны того, кто их понёс.» (the sister card words/害.md already says «со стороны пострадавшего»). Evidence: frozen EN words/被害.md, «harm viewed from the side that suffers it» (perspective, not sight); JMdict 1484350 sense 1, «damage; harm suffered» / 事故や災害、犯罪などによって受ける損害や悪い影響 (j-nihongo entry for 1484350). Confidence: high.
- C2; Minor; comprehensibility; words/被る.md:46-48; The nuance section ends with an English gloss and authoring meta: «Английское толкование «to suffer» в исходном списке слов… составитель… отнесения к уровню N3.» An English-free beginner cannot use «to suffer», and the sentence is about a word-list compiler, not about Japanese. It can also make the こうむる contrast look like an unofficial aside. The needed contrast is already in Russian at lines 42-44. Remedy: delete lines 46-48 and keep the preceding paragraph. Evidence: the card cites JMdict 1484330 senses 1 and 3 (かぶる: wear on the head; take blame) and 1484340 sense 1 (こうむる: suffer/receive); those uses are already stated in Russian above this paragraph. The English sentence depends on a source-list gloss the learner does not have. Confidence: high.
- C3; Minor; comprehensibility and scoped coverage; words/覚ます.md:4,12,16,32-36; The title and H1 lead with «будить», which is the entire title of existing words/起す.md («будить»), and EN keeps those titles apart («wake someone up» vs «awaken; bring out of sleep»). Every 覚ます example is self-waking («Я проснулся», «Малыш проснулся»), and line 38 says not to assume the transitive label means waking another person, but the card never names 起す/起こす or shows 人を起こす. A learner who already has 起す can treat the new word as the same «будить». The 目を/目が contrast with 覚める is clear; this gap is only the other-person verb. Remedy: in the synonyms section, add «[起す](起す.md)（おこす）, обычно 起こす, будит другого человека: 弟を起こす（おとうとをおこす）. 目を覚ます этим не заменяется.» Change the title and H1 lead from «будить; пробуждать» to «выводить из сна; пробуждать», matching the card’s own gloss at line 16 and avoiding an exact copy of the 起す title. Evidence: words/起す.md title and examples (弟を起こした — «я разбудил»); EN words/起す.md title «wake someone up»; JMdict 1206060 sense 1, cited on the card, is general «to awaken», which can include rousing someone, so the defect is the unexplained collision and the undemonstrated other-person use, not a false dictionary sense. Confidence: high.
