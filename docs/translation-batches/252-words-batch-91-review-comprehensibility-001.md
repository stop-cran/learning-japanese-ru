- C1; medium; comprehensibility; words/停車.md:4 (repeat at line 12); The quiz title «остановка транспорта; стоянка (в пути)» teaches the wrong Russian pair. A learner who memorizes the title will not keep a brief vehicle halt apart from a bus stop or from parking. Line 38 and lines 40–41 do separate the action from a bus stop as a place, so the first title segment fights the card's own warning; the parking word is not corrected in the body at all. Remedy: set both the `title` and the H1 to `остановка транспортного средства; краткая остановка (не парковка)`. The kanji card already glosses this word as «остановка транспортного средства».
