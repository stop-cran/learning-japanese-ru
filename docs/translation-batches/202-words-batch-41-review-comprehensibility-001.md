Findings:
- C1; Minor; comprehensibility; words/灰.md:15; «Количество золы задаёт остальное предложение, а не форма самого слова» reverses the relation a beginner needs. «Задаёт» means the amount determines the sentence, so the opening does not teach that quantity comes from context and that 灰 has no special quantity form. Remedy: replace that sentence with «Сколько золы, показывает всё предложение, а не особая форма слова.» Evidence: EN words/灰.md, "The amount is supplied by the sentence"; the Japanese examples do not mark quantity on 灰. Confidence: high.
- C2; Minor; comprehensibility; words/涙.md:18; «Она плакала от радости» translates 涙を流しました with «плакала», the Russian for 泣く. The same card glosses that construction as «проливать слёзы» (line 15) and says 泣く is a related action, not a synonym with the same grammar (line 28). Impact: the main example of 涙を流す teaches the verb the card is trying to keep distinct, and it is a stronger action than shedding tears. Remedy: «Она от радости пролила слёзы.» Evidence: the card's own gloss and 泣く contrast; EN words/涙.md translates the same sentence as "She shed tears of joy," not "she cried." Confidence: high.
- C3; Minor; comprehensibility; words/温暖.md:16; «na-прилагательное» is an unexplained Latin/English term. An English-free beginner cannot tell that «na» means な, unlike the な-прилагательное wording in 清潔.md and 深刻.md in this batch and in 149 other RU word cards (this file is the only «na-прилагательное»). The next clause does show 温暖な, so the form can still be copied. Remedy: «Это な-прилагательное». Evidence: EN words/温暖.md copied "na-adjective" through unadapted; RU words/清潔.md uses «な-прилагательное» for the same class. Confidence: high.
